DOTTY / M3 MACHINE-SCREW VERSION

Use three standard M3 x 8 mm machine screws. Use the NEW BODY AND BEZEL
together; the original M2 bezel and screw positions do not match this set.

Body: 2.8 mm grip holes, 7.2 mm deep, with 0.4 mm entry chamfers.
Bezel: 3.4 mm clearance holes and 6.2 mm head recesses, 1.1 mm deep.
Heads taller than 1.1 mm will stand above the bezel. Head diameter must
fit the 6.2 mm recess. The 58 mm outer diameter leaves room for the
larger heads and sufficient wall thickness around the M3 holes.

These are grip holes: the screws form their threads in the plastic.
They are NOT 3.4 mm through-holes and contain no printed metal-style
threads, nuts, or inserts. Start straight and tighten gently by hand.
Stop if the screw binds; do not use a powered driver. Repeated removal
can wear the plastic. Use 8 mm screws; longer ones may bottom out.

PRINT fit-test.stl FIRST. It has labelled 2.6, 2.7, 2.8 and 2.9 mm holes
inclined at the same 30-degree face angle as the body. Test on your actual
printer/filament; 2.8 mm is the supplied body's starting size. If it is
tight, clear the hole to 2.8 mm by hand before assembly. If it is loose,
use a smaller pilot override and regenerate, or request that variant.

Print body.stl on its flat base and rear heel; bezel.stl is already face
down. Print at 100% in millimeters. Starting profile: 0.4 mm nozzle,
0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 4 top/bottom layers, 15-20% infill. Enable supports
under the overhanging back and inspect USB/internal-tab overhangs.
Keep the integrated heel attached; it is part of the enclosure.

The circular face is now 58 mm across. Display seat, 30-degree viewing
angle, parallel back, USB access and the 8 mm rear foot follow the same
design. Check the actual electronics fit and gentle screen taps before
final assembly. Both the main base and rear foot must touch the desk.
