DOTTY / 40 x 30 x 8 mm BATTERY / M3 / REMOVABLE BOTTOM

PRINT AT 100%, MILLIMETERS. All STL parts are already on the print bed.

Print body.stl with its open underside on the bed, battery-cover.stl with its
flat exterior/countersinks down, and bezel.stl face down. The cover is a final
assembly part, not a disposable support. Review removable supports under the
body's sloping rear, PCB tabs and USB roof. Remove supports before assembly.

Starting settings: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 4 top/bottom layers,
15-20% infill. The 1.2 mm central battery floor is six layers; inspect the slicer
to ensure it is solid. The cover perimeter is 2.4 mm thick. Its screw shoulders
are thin: avoid excess torque. Run the included M3 fit-test coupon first.

Battery envelope: 40 ACROSS x 30 FRONT-TO-BACK x 8 mm THICK.
Minimum clearance pocket: 41 x 31 x 9 mm. Allowance: 0.5 mm each side.
The user's corner lead does not add to the supplied cell dimensions. The well
is open toward the electronics cavity; keep the real lead away from the seam.

Hardware:
  3 x M3 x 8 mm screws for the existing M3 bezel.
  4 x M3 x 8 mm 90-degree COUNTERSUNK / FLAT-HEAD screws for the bottom.
Bottom heads: up to 6.6 mm across; clearance 3.4 mm; plastic pilot 2.8 mm.
Other head styles can protrude under the base. Longer screws are not specified.

Disconnect the battery before opening the cover. Insert it straight through
the underside, route the top-corner lead upward, then connect/test and fit the
bottom plate. Do not bend, compress or clamp the pouch. The four bottom screws
sit outside the cell's full clearance envelope. Confirm the actual connector
and wire routing before tightening. The nominal battery well does not require
removing the display bezel/boards, but real connector access still needs checking.

58 mm face, 30-degree tilt, same M3 bezel. Assembled case is 3 mm taller than
the 34 x 25 x 5 mm battery case. The original curved nose and upper sloped back
remain. Body STL has its assembled bottom cut at z=2.4 mm translated onto the
print bed; assembly.step restores its correct position above the cover.

CAD validation is included in validation.json. Physical fit, screw bite, cover
strength and print-support settings remain prototype checks on your printer.
