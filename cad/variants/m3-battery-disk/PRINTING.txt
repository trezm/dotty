DOTTY / ORIGINAL SLICED PUCK + SEPARATE BATTERY DISK / M3

PRINT AT 100%, MILLIMETERS. The STL orientations are already bed aligned.

Required: puck-body.stl, base-disk.stl, battery-cover.stl, bezel.stl.
Optional fit checks: fit-ring.stl, fit-test.stl (thread grip), countersink-fit.stl.

Colors: ORANGE puck-body and bezel; SLATE base-disk and battery-cover.
Print the groups in separate filament batches; no multi-material unit needed.
The assembled and individual STEP models retain these colors. STL cannot
store colors. The optional fit ring uses orange in the CAD palette.

Puck body: flat sliced bottom down. Use removable supports under the inclined
rear overhang; inspect the USB roof and PCB tabs. Remove supports before fit-up.
Base disk/cup: TOP FACE DOWN as exported, with battery cavity open upward.
Do not turn it upright to print: that would bridge the 40.5 mm pocket roof.
Battery cover: flat exterior and countersinks down, registration lip up.
Bezel: face down as exported, with the wide countersink mouths on the bed.
Countersink fit coupon: same face-down orientation as the bezel.
With its notched edge on the left and the wide mouths facing you, the coupon
has 6.0, 6.2, 6.4, 6.8 mm seats left to right. The bezel uses 6.2 mm.

Starting settings: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 4 top/bottom layers,
15-20% infill. The 1.2 mm battery floor is six layers; check that it is solid.
The wire-channel roof is 1.2 mm thick. The small 3.2 mm wire grooves and 8 mm
passage roofs need slicer inspection. Keep any generated supports removable.
Do not place permanent supports inside wire channels or the battery cavity.

Battery: 40 ACROSS x 30 FRONT-TO-BACK x 8 mm THICK.
Pocket: 40.5 x 30.5 x 8.5 mm.
Clearance is 0.5 mm TOTAL on each axis (0.25 mm each side), not 0.5 per side.
Do not force a tight or swollen pouch into the pocket. Printer error and real
cell dimensions must be checked before assembling the electronics.

Base disk: diameter 66 mm, height 12.2 mm. Battery floor: 1.2 mm.
Puck: 58 mm face, 30-degree tilt, original sliced upper profile, no rear heel.
Overall assembly: about 66 x 66 x 49.41 mm.

Hardware:
  3 x M3 x 16 mm, 90-degree COUNTERSUNK / FLAT-HEAD screws for the stack.
  3 x M3 x 8 mm, 90-degree COUNTERSUNK / FLAT-HEAD screws for the bezel.
  Countersunk screw lengths INCLUDE the head.
Bezel seat: diameter 6.2 -> 3.4 mm, depth 1.4 mm, included angle 90 degrees.
The 2.2 mm bezel retains a 0.8 mm straight hole below the cone.
Assumed bezel head diameter 6.0 mm; top seats about 0.1 mm below the face.
Check your actual screw in the fit coupon first; head diameters vary.
Stack head diameter <=6.6 mm; through holes 3.4 mm; plastic grip holes 2.8 mm.
The stack screws go through cover and disk into the puck, about 3.8 mm bite.
Do not use longer stack screws. Hand-tighten gently; no power driver.

The three underside screws clamp ALL THREE parts, so opening the battery
cover also releases the puck from the disk. Support the puck while opening.
Dry-fit and pre-thread without the battery first. Disconnect power for assembly.
Route the corner lead through either 3.2 x 1.3 mm roof groove and matching
8 x 5 mm passage into the puck. Verify your connector fits. Insert the battery
straight through the underside, connect/test, then install the cover. Keep
wires out of the seams and screws. The cover must close without compressing
the battery. The nominal cell envelope excludes the separately routed lead.

The disk's circular lip has 0.15 mm radial mating clearance. Adjust for your
printer if it binds; it is for alignment, not a press fit. No adhesive is needed
to close the case. Optional thin rubber feet can go under the flat disk.

CAD and mesh checks are in validation.json. Physical fit, hardware tolerances,
print strength, screw bite and slicer support settings remain prototype checks.

Rebuild: .venv/bin/python cad/variants/m3-battery-disk/build.py
