DOTTY / M3 BATTERY VERSION / ORIGINAL PUCK AND COMPACT BASE

Print body.stl upright on its flat bottom. bezel.stl is already face down.
Use the matching M3 bezel and three M3 x 8 mm screws. The fit-test.stl
coupon is the original M3 screw-fit coupon; test it before the full body.
Base pilots are 2.8 mm, bezel clearance 3.4 mm, head recesses 6.2 x 1.1 mm.
Hand-tighten gently. Longer screws may bottom out.

Starting profile: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15-20% infill.
The 1.2 mm battery floor is six layers at 0.2 mm; inspect the sliced file
to ensure that floor is solid. The surrounding interior floor is 1.6 mm
(eight layers), providing insertion/turning clearance. The original curved
front and inclined puck back are retained.
Only the battery base's short rear wall rises to meet its underside; there
is no tall rear block and no vertical cut through the upper puck.
Use removable supports beneath the sloping rear overhang. Review local
supports for the inclined PCB seats and USB roof.
The battery well opens upward and does not have a low bridging roof.
Internal supports must be removable
through the top before assembly. This is not a certified support-free print.

Battery: 34 x 25 x 5 mm, oriented 34 mm ACROSS, 25 mm front to back.
The minimum pocket is 35 mm across x 26 mm front to back x 6 mm high,
with a 1.2 mm bottom skin. The battery center moves 6.9 mm rearward from
the first battery model. Its front edge moves rearward by 11.4 mm.
The well opens into the main cavity for installation and wire routing;
the cell is not sealed into the print. With the boards and bezel removed,
the checked rigid-cell removal path lifts the cell flat to a 14 mm center
height, tilts it 30 degrees to match the display, centers it in the bore,
raises it about 1.5 mm along the display normal and turns it 90 degrees
below the mounting tabs before lifting out. Install in reverse order.
Insert and test-fit the battery before fitting the boards. The dimensions
do not specify the user's wire exit or protection-board geometry. Verify
those against the actual battery, allow slack and insulation, and retain
the cell without compressing it. Do not install it during printing.

The face stays 58 mm diameter at 30 degrees. Its center is 4 mm higher
than the original M3 model. The original upper sloped back and USB access
remain intact. The small battery base extends to y=22.5 mm, with a short
rear riser reaching the puck at about 12.6 mm above the bed. Added base
material is trimmed to the original tilted cylinder at the front and sides.
CAD and mesh checks pass; actual hardware fit, floor stiffness, assembly
access and slicer settings still need a physical check.
