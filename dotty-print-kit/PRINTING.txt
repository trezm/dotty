DOTTY / PARALLEL-BACK PUCK WITH REAR FOOT

This revision slices the back PARALLEL to the display face, creating a
24.2 mm thick puck including the bezel. A horizontal desk slice through
the underside gives it a 30-degree viewing angle. The integrated rear heel
extends the desk contact by 8 mm for better balance.

1. Print fit-ring.stl first to check the display's outline and the three PCB
   support areas. Physical fit is still untested.
2. Print body.stl and bezel.stl at 100%, in millimeters. The body is already
   flat on its main base and heel; the bezel is already face down.
3. Starting profile: PLA or PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls,
   4 top/bottom layers, 15-20% infill. Enable removable supports under the
   overhanging parallel back face. Inspect the USB roof and internal tabs
   too. Supports inside the cavity may start on the printed floor. Keep
   the integrated heel attached; it is NOT a removable print support.
4. Use three M2 x 8 mm pan/socket-head screws (head diameter <= 4.4 mm,
   head height <= 1.1 mm for a flush finish; taller heads may sit proud).
   Hand-thread gently into the 1.7 mm blind pilots. No heat-set inserts.
5. Power switch ON. USB toward the curved-edge opening. Thin insulating foam
   pads go on the support tabs, which must touch bare PCB at mounting areas.
6. Verify the real display/header stack and cable clearance before closing.
   Do not force the assembly or compress the active glass with the bezel.
7. Both the main flat base and rear heel must touch the desk. Optional
   rubber strips under these two areas must have equal thickness.

Nominal circular face: diameter 54 mm.
Front rim height: 9.4 mm. Rear rim height: 36.4 mm.
Overall footprint: approximately 54 x 58.7 mm. Back wall: 2 mm thick.
Body depth normal to face: 22 mm; total including bezel: 24.2 mm.
USB opening: 18 x 10 mm in curved edge.
Integrated heel: 8 mm extension, 32 mm wide, about 4.6 mm high at rear.
Contact depth grows from 26.7 mm to 34.7 mm without enlarging the outline.
Check actual XIAO/header clearance to the back wall before closing.
Balance is modeled, not physically tested: dry-fit and test gentle taps.
Keep the USB cable slack to avoid loading the light enclosure.
USB powered, no battery bay. Firmware remains the same Hello World demo.
Full assembly and customization instructions are in README.md.
