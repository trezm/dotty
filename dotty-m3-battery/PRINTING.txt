DOTTY / M3 BATTERY VERSION / VERTICAL REAR WALL

Print body.stl upright on its flat bottom. bezel.stl is already face down.
Use the matching M3 bezel and three M3 x 8 mm screws. The fit-test.stl
coupon is the original M3 screw-fit coupon; test it before the full body.
Base pilots are 2.8 mm, bezel clearance 3.4 mm, head recesses 6.2 x 1.1 mm.
Hand-tighten gently. Longer screws may bottom out.

Starting profile: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15-20% infill.
The 1.2 mm battery floor is six layers at 0.2 mm; inspect the sliced file
to ensure that floor is solid. The rear exterior wall is vertical and
reaches the bed, removing the old sloping rear exterior overhang.
Review local removable supports for the inclined PCB seats and USB roof.
The battery well opens upward and does not have a low bridging roof.
Internal supports must be removable
through the top before assembly. This is not a certified support-free print.

Battery: 34 x 25 x 5 mm, oriented 34 mm front to back, 25 mm across.
The minimum pocket is 35 x 26 x 6 mm with a 1.2 mm bottom skin.
The well opens into the main cavity for installation and wire routing;
the cell is not sealed into the print. With the boards and bezel removed,
the checked rigid-cell removal path lifts the cell flat to a 20 mm center
height, shifts it 2.3 mm rearward, tilts it 30 degrees to match the display,
then lifts it out along the display normal. Install in reverse order.
Insert and test-fit the battery before fitting the boards. The dimensions
do not specify the user's wire exit or protection-board geometry. Verify
those against the actual battery, allow slack and insulation, and retain
the cell without compressing it. Do not install it during printing.

The face stays 58 mm diameter at 30 degrees. Its center is 4 mm higher
than the original M3 model. The rear vertical wall is behind the assumed
XIAO/header envelope so the USB path and board clearance remain open.
CAD and mesh checks pass; actual hardware fit, floor stiffness, assembly
access and slicer settings still need a physical check.

Rebuild from the project root:
  .venv/bin/python cad/variants/m3-battery/build.py
